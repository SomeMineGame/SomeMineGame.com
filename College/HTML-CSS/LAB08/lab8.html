Livestreaming is becoming increasingly popular online. You first need to pick your platform. The two most popular currently are YouTube and Twitch. After you have your account set up, you need to pick your broadcasting software. The biggest two currently are OBS and Streamlabs. They are both open source and free. After configuring your sources, you can connect your account and go live! You can even expand to more platforms!
